agy-solitaire: 5 Accessible Card Designs

Custom-engineered for an iPhone 16e (Portrait) for a player with monocular vision & floaters.
Classic 2-Color (High-Contrast Red & Deep Black) with zero decorative visual noise.

Accessibility Goal: In portrait Klondike Solitaire on a 390pt screen, cards cascade vertically. The top edge of overlapping cards must remain unmistakable without requiring squinting or confusing floater shadows with small ornate details.
1. Clean Jumbo-Index 2. Top Banner Header 3. Edge-to-Edge Bold 4. High-Contrast Pill 5. Casino Low-Vision
Option 1: Clean Jumbo-Index (Refined Classic)
Recommended Balanced

A direct modernization of standard cards with 250% enlarged corner index (rank + suit stacked vertically). Central area uses clean, bold iconography without tiny scattered pips. Face cards (J, Q, K) feature giant bold letters so there is no visual clutter to confuse floaters.

Option 2: Top Banner Header (Maximum Overlap Clarity)
Best for Tableau Stacking

Features a distinct horizontal header bar across the top of every card (soft red tint for Hearts/Diamonds, soft cool slate for Spades/Clubs). The rank sits on the far left and suit on the far right. In a portrait Solitaire cascade, this entire header is 100% exposed across every stacked card!

Option 3: Edge-to-Edge Bold (Maximum Font Scale)
Largest Numbers

Pushes font size to the absolute physical limit. Rank and suit sit side-by-side in the top-left with ultra-heavy stroke weight. Face cards display an imposing, giant central letter (J, Q, K). Ideal if the user prefers reading by pure number scale.

Option 4: High-Contrast Pill (Solid Inverted Badge)
Maximum Color Contrast

Inverts the index into a solid filled pill (solid red pill for red cards, solid black pill for black cards with crisp white text). This makes the color difference unmistakable from across the room, completely preventing accidental red-on-red or black-on-black tableau drops.

Option 5: Casino Low-Vision (Dual Corner Jumbo)
Traditional Casino Deck

Modeled directly after specialized low-vision large-print decks used in casino senior rooms. Has dual diagonal indices (top-left and bottom-right). Centered pips are enlarged and simplified (e.g. 10 has 4 bold central pips instead of 10 tiny pips to eliminate clutter).
