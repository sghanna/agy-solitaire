Option 2 + Option 3 Hybrids

Combining the Tableau Overlap Visibility of Option 2 with the Maximum Font Scale & Heavy Strokes of Option 3.
Engineered for iPhone 16e Portrait Mode (monocular vision & floater comfort).

Why this combination is so effective: Option 2's top header ensures that as cards stack vertically in a portrait column, 100% of both the number and suit remain exposed. Infusing this with Option 3's ultra-heavy 900-weight typography makes the numbers gigantic and bold enough to resist letter "dropout" when floaters drift across the field of view.
